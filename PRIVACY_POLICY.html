1. Introduction

Shift Work Calendar helps shift workers and teams manage schedules, tasks, machine maintenance, and coworkers offline on your device. We do not operate cloud accounts for this app.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data to our servers. There is no login, no cloud sync, and no analytics SDK in this version. You may store shift data, coworker names, birthdays, tasks, and maintenance records locally on your device.

3. How your data is used

Your data powers calendar views, reminders, statistics, backup/restore, and export features entirely on your device.

4. Permissions

PermissionPurpose
NotificationsOptional reminders you enable
Exact alarmsTimely reminders (inexact fallback if denied)

The app does not request Internet, location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.

5. Backup and export

Backup and export use the system file picker; files are saved only where you choose. Automatic cloud backup excludes the app database and settings.

6. Third parties

This version includes no advertising (AdMob) or third-party analytics.

7. Deletion

Clear app storage or uninstall to remove on-device data. Delete exported files separately if you created them.

8. Security

Data remains on your device under your operating system's protections.

9. Children

The app does not collect personal data online. Supervise minors on shared devices if they enter personal information.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when features change. The effective date will be revised accordingly.
